# Flappy Fofucho vs Mosquitoes
In this short game, you and play as Fofucho an aircraft fighter, and you have one mission: destroy the mosquitoes empire. For that, Fofucho needs to survive for 30 seconds and defeat the evil Big Bad Mosquitoe (AKA BBM)

To move Felpudo up, press the "right button of the mouse" 

To shoot projectiles and kill the mosquitoes, press "J"
